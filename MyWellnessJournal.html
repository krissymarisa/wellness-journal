Backup & Help
Your data is saved inside the downloaded file. Every time you tap Save, a new file downloads with your latest data baked in.

How to save your data

1. Fill in your log and tap Save & Download.
2. A new MyWellnessJournal.html file downloads.
3. Go to netlify.com/drop on your phone or computer.
4. Drag or upload the new file — your Netlify URL stays the same!
5. Open your home screen icon and your data will be there.

Export JSON backup
